For a fixed b the problem is exactly the 2D minimum-area enclosing ellipse of the gnomonic projection onto the tangent plane at b; the outer solve moves b until that ellipse is centred on the tangency point. At the optimum b is an eigenvector of A with σ₀ = 1/√3 ≈ 0.5774 — the panel shows it as a check, it is never imposed.
